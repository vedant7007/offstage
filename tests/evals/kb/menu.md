# HackNova 2026 Food and Menu

All meals are served at the Food Court in Block D. Food is not served inside the auditorium or the labs. Your ticket QR code is your meal coupon; show it at the counter.

## Day 1: Saturday 24 October

- Breakfast, 08:00 to 09:30: idli, vada, sambar, poha, tea and coffee.
- Lunch, 12:30 to 14:00: veg biryani, chicken biryani, paneer butter masala, dal, rice, curd, and gulab jamun.
- Evening snacks, 16:30 to 17:30: samosa and tea.
- Dinner, 20:00 to 21:30, for hackathon participants only: chapati, veg kurma, chilli chicken, jeera rice and ice cream.
- Midnight snacks, 00:30: Maggi, sandwiches, tea and coffee, for hackathon participants.

## Day 2: Sunday 25 October

- Breakfast, 07:30 to 09:00: upma, dosa, chutney, tea and coffee.
- Lunch, 12:30 to 14:00: veg pulao, egg curry, dal tadka, rice, curd and fruit salad.

## Dietary options

Every meal has a vegetarian option. Jain meals are available if you selected Jain as your food preference during registration. Vegan options are available at lunch on request at the counter.

## Allergens

The Day 1 lunch dessert (gulab jamun) contains dairy, and the Day 1 dinner ice cream contains nuts. Ask the counter staff for the allergen card for any dish.

## Drinks

A tea and coffee counter runs 24 hours during the hackathon on the Block B second floor, near Lab 204. Drinking water refill stations are on every floor; bring your own bottle.
