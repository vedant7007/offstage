# HackNova 2026 Rulebook

HackNova 2026 is a two-day tech fest with a 24-hour hackathon, held on 24 and 25 October 2026 at Deccan Institute of Engineering and Technology, Hyderabad.

## Eligibility

The hackathon is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students from any college. Participants must be 18 or older, or confirm guardian consent during registration. A valid college ID card is mandatory at check-in and must be worn at all times on campus.

## Teams

Teams have 2 to 4 members. A person can be part of only one team. Team members can be changed until 18:00 IST on 23 October 2026 from the registration portal. After that, team composition is frozen. Solo participation is not allowed in the hackathon, but solo attendees are welcome at talks and workshops.

## Hackathon timeline

The hackathon starts at 10:00 IST on 24 October and ends at 10:00 IST on 25 October. Final submissions must be made on the submission portal before 10:00 IST on 25 October. Late submissions are not accepted. Shortlisted teams present to judges from 11:30 to 14:30 on 25 October, and results are announced at the closing ceremony at 16:00 in the Main Auditorium.

## Tracks

There are three tracks: AI and ML, Web and Cloud, and Hardware and IoT. Each team picks one track at registration and can change it until the hackathon starts.

## Judging criteria

Projects are scored out of 100: innovation 30 points, technical depth 30 points, impact 20 points, and presentation 20 points. Judges' decisions are final.

## Prizes

First prize is 50,000 INR, second prize is 30,000 INR, and third prize is 15,000 INR. There is also a Best Women-led Team prize of 10,000 INR. Prize money is transferred to the team leader's bank account within 30 days after the event.

## Code and tools

All project code must be written during the hackathon. Open-source libraries and public APIs are allowed with attribution in the README. AI coding assistants are allowed, but their use must be disclosed in the submission. Reusing a project built before the event leads to disqualification.

## Conduct

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Report any incident to a volunteer in a HackNova T-shirt or at the helpdesk; reports are handled confidentially by the organising committee. Alcohol, smoking and vaping are prohibited on campus. Violations can lead to removal from the event without refund.

## Hardware

Bring your own laptop and chargers. The Hardware and IoT track gets a shared kit (Arduino, ESP32, sensors) issued against a college ID at the Lab 101 counter; kits must be returned before 12:00 on 25 October.

## Mentors

Mentors are available in the hackathon labs from 12:00 to 20:00 on 24 October and from 07:00 to 09:30 on 25 October. Book a slot at the mentor desk outside Lab 204.

## Certificates and OD

All participants who check in on both days receive a participation certificate by email within 7 days after the event. Winners receive merit certificates. Every certificate has an ID that can be checked on the public verify page. Students of Deccan Institute get On Duty (OD) attendance through their department: the OD list is sent to HODs by 28 October. Students from other colleges can request an attendance letter at the helpdesk on 25 October.

## Fees and refunds

The hackathon registration fee is 200 INR per person. Talks and workshops are free for registered attendees. Fees are non-refundable, except when the organisers cancel the event, in which case the full fee is refunded within 14 days.
