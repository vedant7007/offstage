# Venue Notes: Deccan Institute of Engineering and Technology

## Rooms

- Main Auditorium: Block A, ground floor, 400 seats. Keynotes, opening at 09:30 on 24 October, and the closing ceremony at 16:00 on 25 October.
- Lab 204: Block B, second floor, 60 seats. AI and ML track workshops and hackathon teams.
- Lab 101: Block B, first floor, 80 seats. Web and Cloud track, and the hardware kit counter.
- Seminar Hall 3: Block C, first floor, 120 seats. Hardware and IoT track talks and panel discussions.

## Getting around

Block A is straight ahead from the main gate. Block B is to the left of Block A, connected by a covered walkway. Block C is behind Block B. The Food Court is in Block D, next to the parking area.

## Accessibility

Wheelchair ramps are at the entrances of Block A and Block B. Block B has a lift near the main staircase that reaches every floor. Accessible washrooms are on the ground floor of Block A and Block B. Seminar Hall 3 in Block C is reachable by a ramp from the Block B walkway. Tell the registration desk if you need help moving between blocks, and a volunteer will accompany you.

## Facilities

Washrooms are on every floor of every block. Drinking water refill stations are on every floor. A quiet room for rest is in Room C-105, and a prayer room is in Room C-104. The helpdesk and registration desk are at the main gate.

## Timings and access

Campus gates open at 08:00 on both days. Non-hackathon attendees must leave by 22:00. Hackathon participants may stay overnight in the Block B labs and the Library reading hall on 24 October. Only Gate 1 (main gate) is open after 20:00.

## Hackathon spaces

Hackathon teams work in Lab 204, Lab 101 and the Block B second floor corridor. The Library reading hall on the first floor of Block D is open overnight for rest.

## Safety

The first aid room is Room G-12, ground floor, Block A. Fire exits are marked on every floor, and the assembly point is the main lawn in front of Block A.
