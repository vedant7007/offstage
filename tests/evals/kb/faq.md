# HackNova 2026 FAQ

## How do I register?

Register on the HackNova event page. Enter your email, verify it with the one-time password (OTP) we send, and fill in your college, department, year and session choices. You get a ticket with a QR code on the page and by email.

## I lost my ticket QR code. What do I do?

Log in to the attendee portal with your email and the OTP to open your ticket again. The helpdesk at the main gate can also look up your registration if you show your college ID.

## When does check-in open?

Check-in opens at 08:30 IST on both days at the registration desk at the main gate and closes at 10:00. Hackathon participants who stay overnight do not need to check in again on day 2, but must keep their badge on.

## Is there a waitlist?

Yes. When a session or the hackathon is full, you are added to the waitlist in the order you registered. If a seat frees up, you are promoted automatically and notified on your registered channel.

## Is there WiFi?

Yes. Connect to the network HackNova-Guest. The password is printed on the back of your badge and is also available at the helpdesk.

## Where can I park?

Two-wheeler parking is behind Block C. There is no car parking for students on campus; cars can use the paid parking on the main road outside Gate 2.

## Is accommodation provided?

No accommodation is provided. Hackathon participants can stay overnight in the hackathon labs and the Library reading hall on the night of 24 October. Talks and workshop attendees must leave campus by 22:00.

## How do I get to the venue?

The nearest metro station is 2 km from the campus. A free shuttle runs from the metro station to the main gate every 30 minutes from 08:00 to 10:30 and from 17:00 to 20:00 on both days.

## Do I get an OD letter?

Deccan Institute students get On Duty attendance through their department; the OD list goes to HODs by 28 October. Students from other colleges can ask for an attendance letter at the helpdesk on 25 October.

## When will I get my certificate?

Participation certificates are emailed within 7 days after the event to everyone who checked in on both days. You can check any certificate ID on the verify page.

## Can I attend only the workshops?

Yes. Talks and workshops are free for registered attendees, and you do not need to join the hackathon to attend them. Workshop seats are limited, so pick your sessions during registration.

## Is there an age limit?

Participants must be 18 or older, or confirm that a parent or guardian has given consent during registration.

## Where is the lost and found?

Lost and found is at the helpdesk next to the registration desk at the main gate.

## Is there medical help on campus?

Yes. The first aid room is Room G-12 on the ground floor of Block A, staffed throughout the event. An ambulance is on call. In an emergency, tell any volunteer immediately.

## Can I charge my laptop and phone?

Every hackathon lab has power strips at each table. The auditorium has charging points along the side walls. Power backup covers all labs.

## How do I contact the organisers?

Visit the helpdesk at the main gate, ask any volunteer in a HackNova T-shirt, or write to help@hacknova.example.

## How do I delete my data?

Use the "delete my data" option in the attendee portal. Personal data is deleted 90 days after the event unless you have requested a certificate.
